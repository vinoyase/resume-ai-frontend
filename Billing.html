Billing Policy

This page explains how billing and subscriptions work for our AI resume builder service.

Subscription

Our service is offered through a recurring subscription. When you purchase a plan, you will be billed automatically according to the selected billing period.

Auto Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless you cancel before the next billing date.

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time. After cancellation, you will continue to have access to the service until the end of the current billing period.

Refund Policy

Due to the nature of digital services, refunds may not be provided after the service has been used. If you experience technical issues, please contact support.

Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely through our payment provider. We do not store your full credit card information on our servers.

Contact

If you have any billing questions, please contact us at: support@smartresumeaip.com
